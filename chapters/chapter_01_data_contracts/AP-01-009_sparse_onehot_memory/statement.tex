        \subsection{Mathematical statement}


For $y_i\in\{0,\ldots,K-1\}$, define
\[
  Y_{ik}=\mathbf 1[y_i=k],\qquad
  Y\bm 1_K=\bm 1_N,\qquad
  y_i=\arg\max_kY_{ik}.
\]
Two value-equivalent label representations have storage costs
\[
  M_{\mathrm{sparse}}=N\,s_{\mathrm{int}},\qquad
  M_{\mathrm{onehot}}=NK\,s_{\mathrm{float}}.
\]

        \subsection{Theory--engineering gap inventory}

        \begin{gapbox}
        \textbf{Explicit gap.} The basis vector $\bm e_{y_i}$ is representation-free, whereas software must choose dense/sparse storage, dtype, layout, and invalid-index behavior.

        \textbf{Implicit gap exposed by the result.} The measured amplification ratio shows that a mathematically convenient expansion can be an engineering regression even when values are exact.
        \end{gapbox}

        \subsection{Acceptance evidence}
