\problemstart{AP-02-002}{Stable Softplus Across Extreme Inputs}{Difficulty 3/5}{Chapter 2 \textbullet\ numpy}{Evaluate $\log(1+e^x)$ without overflow or loss of the small positive tail.}

        \subsection{Mathematical statement}


The softplus function admits the exact identity
\[
\begin{aligned}
\operatorname{softplus}(x)
  &=\log(1+e^x)\\
  &=\max(x,0)+\log\!\left(1+e^{-|x|}\right).
\end{aligned}
\]
The second line bounds the exponential argument by zero and uses a log-one-plus
operation designed for small increments.

        \subsection{Theory--engineering gap inventory}

        \begin{gapbox}
        \textbf{Explicit gap.} Algebraic equality over reals does not preserve overflow, underflow, or relative accuracy after a finite sequence of rounded operations.

        \textbf{Implicit gap exposed by the result.} The $\pm1000$ fixture makes the naive expression overflow while the stable expression stays finite and retains a nonnegative negative-tail value.
        \end{gapbox}
