\problemstart{AP-02-006}{Label Smoothing Is a Convention, Not a Slogan}{Difficulty 3/5}{Chapter 2 \textbullet\ numpy}{Implement one precisely stated smoothing convention and observe its entropy and gradient consequences.}

        \subsection{Mathematical statement}


This problem fixes the all-class uniform-mixture convention
\[
  \tilde{\bm y}_i=(1-\varepsilon)\bm e_{y_i}
  +\frac{\varepsilon}{K}\bm 1_K,
  \qquad 0\le\varepsilon\le1.
\]
Thus
\[
\tilde y_{i,y_i}=1-\varepsilon+\varepsilon/K,
\qquad
\tilde y_{i,k}=\varepsilon/K\;(k\ne y_i).
\]

        \subsection{Theory--engineering gap inventory}

        \begin{gapbox}
        \textbf{Explicit gap.} Different APIs distribute smoothing mass over all classes or only incorrect classes; the phrase ``label smoothing'' alone is mathematically under-specified.

        \textbf{Implicit gap exposed by the result.} At identical logits, increasing epsilon raises target entropy and changes the gradient vector, revealing that smoothing is part of the objective rather than data decoration.
        \end{gapbox}

        \subsection{Acceptance evidence}

        \begin{evidencebox}
